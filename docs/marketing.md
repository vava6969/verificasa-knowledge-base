# Marketing

## Instagram: @verificasaro

**Goal (next 3 months):** gain new followers before launch.

### Posting system (from 23 Oct 2026)

- **3 carousel posts a day** (5–8 slides) at **09:03, 15:00 and 19:05** Romania time, every day including weekends.
- Content rotates between **renters, buyers and owners/agencies**: home-buying tips, choosing an agent, property tips, AI and ChatGPT tools for real estate, and VerifiCasa promo memes.
- Every post gets a fresh hook, idea and design.
- Reference styles: Wolf Media, Swela Realty, ai_deb, Joel Ooreofe, piyush.glitch.

### Approval flow

- Claude runs as a scheduled marketing and news agent.
- A **preview email goes to marketing@verificasa.ro about 1 hour before each post**. No reply means the post is approved.
- **Breaking news** posts go out right away without approval, branded **"VerifiCasa Știri Imobiliare · Ediție specială"**. They need two serious sources, with credit. Private persons are never named.
- Instagram is connected through **Composio**. Simple comments can get automatic replies. The marketing team handles other comments and DMs.

### Visuals

- **Higgsfield** generates the slides. Budget is **10–15 credits/day** (up to +10 when needed). Keep spend low while keeping quality high.
- Published slides must match the Higgsfield output exactly. If something is wrong, regenerate instead of retouching.

## Video

- Promo and ad videos are in **Romanian**. A motion-graphics editor (English-speaking) and a marketing videographer were hired.
- A **30-second promo ad** with a female presenter was filmed for the app and site launch.
- **Explainer style** (from 26 Oct 2026), based on imoreview.ro: animated low-poly 3D, cartoon-like scenes (blocks, cracks, buildings, cutaways), visual storytelling rather than text on a background, lots of VerifiCasa branding, a chapter progress bar at the top, bold keyword captions with one colored word, and callout labels with leader lines.
- **Cinematic listing videos:** turning listing photos into walkthrough videos with Higgsfield (dolly-in, pan, push-in, drift, text overlays). First test was a one-room apartment in Iași. Lesson: check model cost on a single image before running a batch.

## Launch

- Build an audience before launch.
- Gifts for the first 5 real-estate agencies that join.
