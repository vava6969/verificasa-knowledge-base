# Product: what VerifiCasa does

VerifiCasa is a **website** (verificasa.ro) plus a **mobile app** (iOS and Android) for finding, verifying and presenting rental and sale properties in Romania.

## Core features

- **Verified listings.** Every listing is moderated by the VerifiCasa team before it goes live.
- **Property history and transparency.** Users can search the history of an apartment or house. Background checks (developer, seismic risk, building permits, area pricing) are provided through the partner **Residence Vertical** (residencevertical.ro).
- **3D / 360° virtual tours.** VerifiCasa builds the tours for listings itself, using its own scanning system (~50 MB per tour). See [3d-tours.md](3d-tours.md).
- **Secure messaging** between renters/buyers and owners/agents.
- **Newsletter** on the site.
- **Upgrade plans** (monthly subscription) that unlock all upgrades, including access to the 3D tour app. See [pricing.md](pricing.md).

## Who it serves

- **Renters and buyers:** verified listings, property history, immersive tours, and (planned) free virtual staging so they can picture a furnished space.
- **Owners:** list properties with verification and professional 3D presentation.
- **Real-estate agencies and agents:** an all-in-one toolkit with 3D/2D/Dollhouse tours, measurements, floor plans, a document scanner and e-signature, plus a **built-in CRM** (planned). Launch promotion: gifts for the **first 5 agencies** to join.

## Operating philosophy

VerifiCasa is designed to run **as automated as possible, ideally with no employees needed** for day-to-day operations. Marketing, content and many workflows are automated with AI agents.
