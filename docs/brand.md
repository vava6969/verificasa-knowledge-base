# Brand

## Name and tagline

- **VerifiCasa**, written with "Casa" in green in the wordmark
- Tagline: **"Chirii verificate online. Fără surprize neplăcute."**

## Logo

A black/white **"VC" monogram with a house icon**, next to the "VerifiCasa" wordmark.

## Colors

| Use | Hex |
|---|---|
| Website green accent | `#1F7048` |
| Website background | white / light |
| 360° tour app background | `#0D131C` (near-black navy) |
| 360° tour app accent | `#22C55E` |
| 360° tour app text | `#F9FAFB` |

## Design rules for marketing

- Designs should look **human-made, not AI-generated**, with **no emoji on images**.
- Brand colors can make up about **40%** of a design when another palette works better. The green palette is used only in some posts.
- Every Instagram slide has the **real VerifiCasa logo bottom-left** and a small Instagram glyph next to **@verificasaro**.
- Credit every source on the post (site name, logo or a headline screenshot) and add short citations in the caption. Double-check every fact before publishing.
